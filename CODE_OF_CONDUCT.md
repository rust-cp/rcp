# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion on the work. Criticism
belongs on the change, not the person. Give people room to ask questions and
to correct a mistake.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, and sexualized language are not welcome, nor is publishing
someone else's private information. Do not use issues or pull requests to
pressure a person, and do not retaliate against someone who reports a problem.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through a private channel.
Where private vulnerability reporting is available, the form linked from
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) is one such channel; say that the report is a
conduct concern so it is not handled as a vulnerability. Do not file a public
issue for a conduct report.

The repository owner will acknowledge a report within 5 working days and will
say what action was taken. That can include a warning, an edit, or a block.
Reports are kept private aside from the people needed to act on them.
